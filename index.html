About Kaveri

People-centred HR, grounded in real-world experience

My background is in HR, with over 20 years' experience spanning Healthcare, Higher Education and international consultancy. Over time I've built deep expertise across workforce planning, talent management, employee relations, leadership development and people strategy.

I work with professionals, managers and organisations who want clear, trustworthy guidance — whether that's navigating a career, developing as a leader, or strengthening how an organisation supports its people.

  • Practical advice rooted in two decades of HR leadership
  • Experience across regulated and complex environments
  • A calm, professional and confidential approach
