My Background

A career built around people

My background is in HR, with over 20 years' experience across Healthcare, Higher Education and international consultancy. Throughout that journey I've focused on one thing above all: helping people and organisations succeed together.

Over time, I've built experience in workforce planning, talent management, employee relations, leadership development and people strategy. That breadth means I can see challenges from multiple angles — the individual, the team and the wider organisation.

Today, I bring that experience to professionals seeking direction, leaders looking to grow, and organisations that want practical, trustworthy HR support.
