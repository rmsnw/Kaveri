Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information is collected through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information collected

The only personal information actively collected through this website is the information you choose to provide via the enquiry form, which may include your name, email address, phone number and the details of your enquiry.

How your information is used

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange an initial conversation.
  • To provide the coaching, consultancy or advisory services you request.
  • To keep records of our correspondence relating to your enquiry.

Your information is used only for the purposes above. It is never sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

Lawful basis

Information submitted through the enquiry form is processed on the basis of your consent and/or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a possible agreement.

Data retention

Enquiry information is retained only for as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to deliver agreed services and meet any legal or record-keeping obligations. After that, it is securely deleted.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to the personal information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to withdraw your consent at any time. To make a request, please get in touch using the contact details below.

Cookies & analytics

This website aims to keep tracking to a minimum. If analytics or cookies are introduced in future, this policy will be updated to explain what is used and why.
